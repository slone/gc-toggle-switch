<gc-toggle-switch>

Theme:

An accessible, form-associated toggle switch built as a native Custom Element.

Horizontal (default)

Airplane mode (aria-label)

Click, press Space or Enter, or drag the thumb.

Vertical ("on" = thumb at the top)

A row of bits, binary-converter style:

10 10 10 10 10 10 10 10

Colours, sizes and parts

State indicators

ONOFF

Indicators do not rely on colour: helpful for colour-blind or low-vision users.

Right-to-left (dir="rtl")

In RTL, "on" puts the thumb on the left (line end), and dragging follows that direction.

Busy (async action)

While busy is set, a spinner appears in the thumb and clicks are ignored.

Cancelling a toggle (preventDefault())

While "Allow changes" is off, a click listener calls preventDefault(): the switch does not toggle and no change is fired.
